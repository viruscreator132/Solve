FAQ's

Common Homeowner Questions

How long does a standard Calgary roof replacement take?

Most residential replacements are completed in 1 to 2 days, including full tear-off, underlayment, shingle installation, and magnetic nail cleanup.

Do you work directly with Alberta home insurance providers?

Yes. We document hail and wind damage thoroughly and coordinate directly with adjusters so your full scope is approved properly.

What does your Lifetime Workmanship Warranty cover?

It covers installation workmanship including flashing, valleys, vents, and shingle fastening—giving you permanent peace of mind.
